## Real execution

Canonical case `local-samantha-v1` uses the surviving `/private/tmp/stt-t14-assets/input.json` directly. Its 112,640 mono Float32 frames at 16 kHz cover 7.040 s starting at 5000 ms. Verified little-endian PCM SHA-256 `319c127d3e77fc3d425cd93a78d6df167e3c6fdfc9daa8ea26748a1aace42a88`; source SHA-256 `a851e66db61c798f7ea34c1cc775a78fc078d4b202cd45a0260670cd5a662424`. No hint, temperature zero, timestamps and partials enabled. All quoted results are from the final CLI. Model weights, synthetic PCM, transcript outputs and traces are outside Git.

On Mac16,8 (Apple M4 Pro, 24 GiB), all successful native repetitions produced three nonempty segments at **5000–7320**, **7600–9760** and **9960–12040 ms**. The readable outputs contain the exact synthetic English text. Native stderr confirms embedded Metal, MTL0 model storage and BLAS. The OS denied a direct TCP connection with EPERM. Each offline invocation used fresh HOME, CF home, XDG cache and temp directories and ran outside the checkout.
